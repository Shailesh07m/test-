Property Management System

The heart of Nexstay HMS. Manage every reservation, guest folio, and room status from a single intuitive screen — designed for speed even during peak season rush.

  • Visual drag-and-drop booking calendar with room-wise view
  • Individual, group & corporate booking flows
  • Express check-in/out with digital signature capture
  • Multi-room, multi-guest folio management
  • Early check-in / late check-out & waitlist handling
  • Daily arrival/departure reports & night audit automation
  • Walk-in booking with instant room assignment
Channel Manager

Stop managing OTAs manually. Nexstay syncs rates and inventory across all channels in real time — eliminating double bookings and maximizing revenue.

  • 100+ OTA integrations: Booking.com, Airbnb, Agoda, MakeMyTrip
  • Real-time 2-way sync of rates and availability
  • Bulk rate updates with seasonal pricing rules
  • Channel-wise revenue and performance analytics
  • Restriction management: min stay, stop sell, CTA/CTD
  • Auto rate parity monitoring and alerts
POS & Food & Beverage

A complete restaurant management solution built for hotels. From table ordering to room service to bar billing — all linked directly to the guest folio.

  • Touch-screen POS for dining, bar, pool, and room service
  • KOT/BOT printing to kitchen and bar printers
  • Table reservation and seat management
  • Inventory & recipe costing for F&B profitability
  • Post charges directly to guest room folio
  • GST-compliant billing with split payment support
Accounts & Billing

Replace manual ledgers with a fully automated financial system integrated into every hotel operation — from front desk to F&B to housekeeping supplies.

  • Automated revenue posting and night audit
  • GST invoicing and e-invoicing compliance (IRN generation)
  • Accounts receivable & city ledger management
  • Multi-currency billing for international guests
  • Tally ERP integration for accounting teams
  • P&L, trial balance, and audit-ready financial reports
Guest CRM & Loyalty

Know your guests deeply. Build lasting relationships, reward loyalty, and automate personalized communication that drives repeat bookings.

  • Complete guest profile with full stay history and preferences
  • Automated pre-arrival and post-stay emails/SMS/WhatsApp
  • Loyalty points, membership tiers, and reward redemption
  • Guest feedback and NPS survey automation
  • Birthday, anniversary, and special occasion offer automation
  • Segmented marketing campaigns by guest type
Housekeeping Management

Give your housekeeping team a superpower. Automate task assignment, track real-time room status, and reduce supervisor workload with intelligent dashboards.

  • Auto-assign rooms to housekeepers based on floor and workload
  • Real-time room status: Vacant Dirty, Occupied, Inspected, Clean
  • Mobile app for housekeepers to update status on the go
  • Supervisor inspection checklist and approval workflow
  • Lost & found item management
  • Minibar consumption tracking linked to folio
